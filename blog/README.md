This directory contains English editorial material about what FrameBridge does, how it is evaluated and what remains to improve.

The first [article draft](2026-10-25-why-framebridge.md) introduces persistent terminals, real console interaction and selected Linux nodes. It remains unpublished. Its editorial identity is the FrameBridge project; legal ownership and attribution remain in [COPYRIGHT.md](../COPYRIGHT.md).

Describe reproducible work, observed results and current limitations. Distinguish the accepted RC7 baseline from the separate community candidate and its unfinished release checks. Lead with capabilities and useful workflows rather than founder biography.

Use complete authentic screen captures with approved demonstration content. Identify conceptual animations as illustrations. A fresh real recording of the community candidate remains to be produced and checked.
